## Revision Notes

This version moves away from concept explanation and toward shorter beat-ready phrasing, hard stops, internal rhymes, double rhymes, and sectioned end-rhyme zones. The core is sharpened: not "I do not know where to go," but "I know where to go, yet cannot afford to stay somewhere comfortable."
